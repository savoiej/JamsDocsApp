      Four steps, on your site.

      We do the legwork in your building, so the drawings match what’s really in the walls and racks.

      Step 1

      Trace

      We walk the site with a tone and probe and follow every cable from the wall plate to the rack.

      Step 2

      Document

      We draw what we found: floor plans, signal flow, rack elevations and the cable schedule.

      Step 3

      Label

      Proper printed labels on both ends of every cable, on each port and on each rack, matching the drawings.

      Hand over

      You get the PDF drawing set, the cable schedule as a spreadsheet and the label files. Changes come as a new revision.

      Questions.

      Can you document a site that’s already built?

      Yes, that’s our main work. We walk the site, trace what’s there and produce as-built drawings, rack elevations, network documentation and labels.

      What do I actually receive?

      A PDF drawing set with your title block, the cable schedule as a spreadsheet (CSV), print-ready label files, and the labels installed on site. Changes later come as a new revision.

      Will you disrupt our day?

      We plan the site walk with you and can work after hours. Tracing is quick per cable; we only unplug something when you’ve said it’s OK.

      How is the work priced?

      Documentation gets a fixed price after a short scope call. Service contracts are billed on a regular cycle or as a block of prepaid hours.

      Do you install equipment too?

      Yes. We can support your installer, or handle installs and on-site work under a service contract.

      Do I need to use your app?

      No. You receive finished documents. The app is optional, for teams that want to do their own drawings.
