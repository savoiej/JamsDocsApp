    How the app works

    Draw it once. Everything else follows.

    Nothing gets retyped. The device you place on the drawing is the line on the proposal, the task on the install board and the asset on the service ticket.

    1. 1

      Design

      Place gear from the product library and wire it port to port.

    2. 2

      BOM

      Quantities, cables and connectors count themselves from the sheet.

    3. 3

      Proposal

      Price it, add labor, send it, and get it signed online.

    4. 4

      Project

      Signed work becomes tasks, a schedule and a budget.

    5. 5

      Service

      Techs open the as-built on site and log every visit.
