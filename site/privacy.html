Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Jams Docs and Designs collects through jamsdocsanddesigns.com, the SITREP app and our WhatsApp business number, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Jams Docs and Designs is an AV and network documentation, design and support business based in Hawaii, USA. We run this website and the SITREP app (app.jamsdocsanddesigns.com), which our team and clients use to manage drawings, projects, service work and client messages. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Jams Docs and Designs.

What we collect

When you send a request from the website

The request form asks for your name, email address, and optionally your company, phone number, the services you need, the size and timing of the job, and a description of your space. We receive this as an email.

When you visit the website

We use Google Tag Manager, which may load Google analytics tools that set cookies and collect information such as the pages you view, your browser and device type, an approximate location based on your IP address, and how you arrived at the site. We use this only to understand how the site is used.

When you use the SITREP app

If you have a SITREP account, we store your name, email address and sign-in details, along with the work you and our team enter: client and contact details, project and site information, drawings, photos, equipment lists, schedules, service records, proposals and invoices.

When you pay an invoice

Card and bank payments are handled by Stripe. We receive confirmation of the payment and limited details such as the card brand and last four digits. We never see or store your full card or bank account number.

When you message us

If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we keep the messages and the contact details that come with them so our team can reply and keep a record of the job. See the WhatsApp section below.

WhatsApp messages

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform, provided by Meta, to talk with clients. When you message our WhatsApp business number:

  • We receive your WhatsApp phone number, your WhatsApp profile name, the content of your messages and their date and time. Photos, files, voice notes and locations you send are noted in the conversation; you may be asked to share them another way.
  • Messages are stored in the client inbox of the SITREP app, matched to your client record when your number is on file, so the people helping you can see the history.
  • Only our office staff can read client messages. Field technicians do not have access to them.
  • We reply to the messages you send us. We do not send marketing messages on WhatsApp unless you have asked to receive them.
  • Meta processes these messages under its own terms and privacy policy. See WhatsApp Business Policy and Meta Privacy Policy.

To stop receiving WhatsApp messages from us, reply STOP or tell us in the chat, and we will not message you again unless you contact us first.

How we use your information

  • To answer your requests, prepare quotes and proposals, and schedule and carry out work.
  • To create and keep documentation for your systems, such as drawing sets, labels and as-built records.
  • To send invoices, take payments and keep business records.
  • To communicate with you about your projects and service contracts.
  • To run, secure and improve the website and the SITREP app.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your client data or messages for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business, and only as needed for the service they provide:

  • Vercel hosts the website and the SITREP app.
  • Supabase stores SITREP account data, project data and client messages.
  • Resend delivers website request emails to our inbox.
  • Microsoft 365 hosts our business email.
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) carries WhatsApp messages between you and us.
  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Google provides Google Tag Manager and website analytics, and the fonts used on this site.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a sale or transfer of the business, in which case this policy would continue to apply.

How long we keep it

We keep project records, documentation and messages for as long as we work with you and for a reasonable time afterwards, because as-built records are often needed years later. Invoices and payment records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require. Website requests that do not lead to work are deleted within two years. You can ask us to delete your information sooner, as described below.

How we protect it

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with providers that encrypt it at rest. Access to the SITREP app requires a signed-in account, and permissions limit who can see what: for example, technicians cannot see prices, payments or client messages. No system is perfectly secure, so we also limit what we collect to what we need.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can ask us to:

  • show you the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your personal information, including your WhatsApp conversation history with us;
  • stop contacting you.

To request deletion, email info@jamsdocsanddesigns.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the email address or phone number you used with us, or send "Delete my data" in our WhatsApp chat. We will confirm when it is done, normally within 30 days. We may keep records we are legally required to keep, such as invoices.

You can also block cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's analytics opt-out add-on. The site still works without them.

Children

Our services are for businesses and homeowners. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will tell SITREP account holders by email or in the app.

Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy: info@jamsdocsanddesigns.com.
